ABOUT THE DEMO

Real memories. A rehearsed conversation.

The replay chat is written for this demo and runs at a steady, visible pace. The clip links come from sjathedude’s own channel and play through the original host. Illustrated artwork is original to Callback and is not gameplay footage.

The account-free replay uses server-side neural retrieval with a labeled local fallback. Connected live channels use server-side neural embeddings. Demo time is compressed; live cooldowns are at least one minute.
